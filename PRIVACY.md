# Privacy Policy: Magic Theme for Phocas

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Magic Theme for Phocas ("the extension") changes how Phocas BI pages look in your own browser. It is an unofficial, independent project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Phocas Software.

## What the extension collects

Nothing. The extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information, browsing history, page content or business data. It has no servers, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network requests of its own.

## What the extension stores, and where

A few preferences are saved **only in your own browser**, using Chrome's local extension storage and the page's local storage:

- whether the theme is on or off
- any extra site addresses you add on the Options page
- your personal column order and dimension order for grids you rearrange

This data never leaves your computer. You can clear it at any time by removing the extension, or by using "Reset columns" and "Reset order" in the extension.

## Page access

The extension runs on `*.phocassoftware.com` pages, and on other sites only when you add them on the Options page and approve Chrome's permission prompt. On those pages it restyles the interface and adds conveniences (for example search boxes in menus). It reads page content only to change how it is displayed, and nothing is sent anywhere. It does not run on Phocas administration or designer pages.
